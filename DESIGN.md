# openmAIc Design

## Product Goals
- Warm, inviting, and calm.
- Frictionless: minimal choices, fast flow.
- “Podcast-ready” feel for audio playback and exports.

## Visual System
- **Typography**: Geist for UI; Geist Mono for logo.
- **Color**: warm cream background, peach primary, soft warm neutrals.
- **Radius**: large and rounded (`--radius: 1.25rem`).
- **Shadows**: soft, warm drop shadows.

## Layout & Components
- **Home**: centered card with mode selector, input, and CTA.
- **Session**: question + record/confirm flow, timeline on right.
- **Summary**: export-focused modal.

## Interaction Principles
- Keep primary CTA obvious.
- Avoid unnecessary toggles.
- Gentle animations only (subtle fade/slide).

## Accessibility
- Visible focus styles.
- Readable text contrast.
- Large tap targets for recording and exports.
